# About me

Hi, I'm Shiva Om Makaju.
I’m a researcher and data analyst specializing in forage and bioenergy crop improvement through plant breeding, quantitative genetics, and data-driven genomic approaches. My work integrates field experimentation, multi-environment analysis, and molecular genomics to enhance crop resilience and yield under environmental stress.
🔬 Research Interests:
•	Genomic selection, GWAS, and QTL mapping in perennial bioenergy crops
•	Genetic and physiological basis of drought and temperature stress tolerance
•	Multi-environment trials, yield stability, and genotype × environment interactions
•	Integrating transcriptomics and phenomics for predictive breeding
💻 Technical Expertise:
•	Statistical & Genomic Tools: R, SAS, GenStat, JoinMap, MapQTL, Agrobase, Genovix, Tableau
•	Field & Laboratory: Greenhouse and multi-location field trial design, tissue sampling, RNA-seq analysis
•	Equipment: Wintersteiger forage harvesters
🌱 Current Projects:
•	Drought Adaptation Index (DAI) development for switchgrass germplasm
•	Transcriptomic profiling of heat and freezing tolerance in perennial ryegrass
•	Comparative genomic prediction pipelines for bioenergy crops
